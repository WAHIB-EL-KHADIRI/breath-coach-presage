# Demo video script (≈ 1 min 45 s) — real camera, no simulation

**Before recording:** valid Presage key, good front light, laptop at eye level, close other camera apps, 1366×768 or 1920×1080 window,
Windows camera access on. Do a dry run first. **Do not use the SIMULATED mode in this video**; if SIMULATED appears on screen, retake.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the app. Welcome screen, orb breathing. | "Breath Coach is a quiet breathing coach. It uses my laptop camera and Presage SmartSpectra to measure my breathing rate." |
| 0:10 | Tick the consent box, point at the camera/privacy lines, Continue. | "Consent comes first. The camera opens only when I press Start, and nothing is recorded." |
| 0:20 | Paste key, tick Remember (if available), Save. Show framing tips. | "My key can stay on this machine, protected by Windows, or be forgotten on close." |
| 0:30 | Press **Start session**. Camera preview appears, status "Starting…" → "Warming up". | "The camera opens now — once. The engine warms up for thirty seconds and shows no number until then." |
| 0:40 | Point at the dashes and the warm-up bar. | "Warm-up progress is just elapsed time, not confidence." |
| 0:55 | Reading appears; point at **Measured breathing rate**, confidence, then **Guide rhythm**. Show the wave moving. | "This is my measured average. This is the guide's rhythm — a separate thing, clamped between six and twelve." |
| 1:10 | Move out of frame (or cover the lens briefly): "Signal lost", dashes, guide returns to default. | "If the signal drops, the number disappears — it never shows an old value as live — and the guide falls back to a default pace." |
| 1:25 | Toggle preview off, still measuring. | "Hiding the preview doesn't stop the measurement." |
| 1:30 | Press **Stop**. Camera light goes out. | "Stop releases the camera immediately." |
| 1:38 | Footer: relaxation, not medical. | "It's for relaxation, not a medical device. Thanks for watching." |

Honest-capture checklist: show the real camera light; keep the app window and OS camera indicator visible when stopping;
no edits that fake readings.
